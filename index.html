About the group

CMLDG started as a small discussion round inside our working group. We read papers, try out tools, and talk through problems where machine learning meets chemistry: property prediction, molecular representations, reaction outcomes, force fields and more.

Now we are opening it up. Each session has one topic and one moderator who guides the discussion. You do not need to present anything to take part.
